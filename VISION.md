# Vision 2045

## Mission

To democratize access to enterprise-grade learning, simulation, AI mentorship, and verified skills so learners can move from knowledge to real capability.

## Vision

By 2045, OmSaravanaBhava becomes a global capability platform where learners can study, practise, simulate, certify, build portfolios, and connect to career opportunities through AI-native learning experiences.

## Core principles

1. **Competence by practice** — skills must be demonstrated, not merely claimed.
2. **AI as mentor** — AI supports learning, reflection, practice, and growth.
3. **Global accessibility** — learning must work across countries, languages, bandwidth levels, and devices.
4. **Human ownership** — learners own their progress, portfolio, and competency record.
5. **Integrity first** — certification, assessment, and AI guidance must prioritize accuracy and trust.
6. **Single source of truth** — content should be reusable across web, mobile, PDF, AI tutor, labs, and assessments.
